    Support

    Small practices for heavy weeks, and when to ask for more help.

    Guided breathing

    Four seconds in, six seconds out. Two minutes is enough to settle your nervous system.

    Ready when you are

    Work in blocks

    Study for 50 minutes, then rest for 10 away from your phone. Stop at a fixed time each evening.

    Protect your sleep

    Sleep is when memory consolidates. A steady wake-up time helps more than a late night of cramming.

    Plan tonight

    Write tomorrow's first task before bed. A clear first step makes starting much easier.

    When to reach out

    If low mood or stress lasts more than a couple of weeks, or is getting in the way of eating, sleeping or studying, speak to a campus counsellor, a doctor or someone you trust. You do not need to wait for a crisis. If you ever feel unsafe, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line right away.
